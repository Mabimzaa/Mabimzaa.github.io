What I do

My three areas of work, from open-source investigation to awareness training.

My specialty

Digital footprint audit

I run the investigation an attacker would carry out before targeting you, using open sources only. You get a clear report: what is exposed, what it enables, and how to fix it.

  • Leaked credentials and data
  • Exposure of executives and staff
  • Visible infrastructure: domains, subdomains, services
  • Impersonation and social engineering risks
  • Prioritised action plan

Security audit

Testing of your website, access points and configuration, within a defined scope authorised in writing.

Support and awareness training

Putting good practices in place, following up on fixes, and awareness sessions for your teams (phishing, passwords, social media).

How an engagement works

Nothing starts without your written agreement on the scope.

  1. Scoping

    We define the scope and goals together, then sign an authorisation to proceed.

  2. Collection

    Open-source research or technical testing, depending on the engagement, without disrupting your business.

  3. Analysis

    Every finding is verified and ranked by its real risk to your company.

  4. Debrief

    A report a non-specialist can read, walked through with you, with the priority fixes.
